# Communities

Communities are spaces that dicate the rules of who can accept certain shifts, you can join and leave communities. You can offer your shift to a community and you will be offered shifts from people in your community.

- Users can create a community
- Users can invite other people to a community using a code that will be available.

## Pages

- Dashboard (all your stuff)
- Communities
    - List of all communities your are in, as well as a way to input community codes
    - Ways to create new communities, you can create them and leave them, if no one is in a community then it is deleted, there is no owner
    - Each community has their own page
        - Community information like the name, (optional fields like company name, address)
        - In the page it has all the members
            - You can direct request members, so the shift is only offered to that particular person (the dashboard shift row must be updated to support this and make it easy to view whether its a public shift or a shift specific for you as well as if you have any offers and who they are for)

Search fields for communities and members
